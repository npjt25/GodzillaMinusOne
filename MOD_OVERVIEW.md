# Godzilla Minus One — Minecraft Forge mod (overview for context)

**What it is:** A Minecraft Java Edition mod for **Forge 1.20.1 (47.x)**, using **GeckoLib 4.x** for animated models. It ports GojiDraw's Bedrock add-on "Godzilla Minus One" V3 to Java and adds a lot of new content from the film *Godzilla Minus One* (2023). Optional support for **Walkers / ReMorphed** (play as Godzilla). Built and tested with GitHub Actions (build + server-side game tests + a scripted run of the real client on a virtual display, with checks and screenshots). Source: github.com/npjt25/GodzillaMinusOne (branch `claude/admiring-euler-xygs92`).

## Godzilla (`godzillaminusone:godzilla`)
- Boss mob, GojiDraw's model scaled to ~45 blocks tall, ~76 long. Phases: emerge from ground → roar → active → collapse → dead → regenerate.
- Roar heard up to 3000 blocks away (louder as you get closer); from 200 blocks on, listeners hear distant-roar recordings instead (alternating). Roars every 2 min (configurable).
- Radiation aura within 16 blocks gives the mod's own **Radiation** effect (trefoil icon; no on-screen warning); a full **hazmat suit** blocks it. Footsteps shake the camera, hurt nearby mobs and crush footprints into the ground.
- Walking, destroys **everything in his path at Y ≥ 60** (ground, trees, buildings; not liquids/bedrock) and **nothing below 60** (`walkBreaksFromY`, footprints too; mob and played Godzilla); **buildings topple over** as he walks through/swipes them (falling blocks).
- Knocks tall buildings (≥ half his ~45-block height, 23) down to a quarter (11) when nothing else to fight, then rests `demolitionIntervalSeconds` (20) before the next.
- Melee combo: two claw swipes, a full-body spin with a whip-snap tail; a real bite (lunge + jaw snap, bite sound), stomps below, tail swing behind. Claws tear through what's built where they rake; the tail knocks away what's built round him (low, out to 30/38 blocks). His blows also break the rest of the Building Blocks (`Destruction.godzillaBreaks`: terracotta incl. glazed, mud family, stone/granite/diorite/andesite/tuff/deepslate, logs and wood incl. stripped, sandstone, basalt, blackstone, end stone); the ground proper (dirt, sand, gravel...) is left.
- Only breathes at targets **≥75 blocks** away (`minTargetDistance`); closer ones get melee. After the beam he roars once (full roar) and goes back to normal.
- **Atomic breath:** dorsal plates light up blue tail-to-neck, 4-second in-breath while his **mouth glows blue, brighter until it's white-hot just before firing**; he rears up and fires. His **head turns to aim** (left/right up to 50°, up/down) and the beam comes **out between his jaws**. Plates slam down as the beam fires. **The beam stops at the first block in its way** (server: damage and blast there; client: `updateBeamReach` clips it each tick, the beam bone drawn only that far, with a blue-white impact there, `Fx.beamImpact`).
- 2 s after impact, a **nuclear blast**: kills nearby, ~96-wide crater, blast wave, **levels land above Y 65 within 60 blocks** (no drops), and **ruins everything out to 100 blocks** (burnt bare trees, charred trunks, broken glass, holed walls/roofs, cracked stone, dead crops, fires). Black rain particles.
- Breath recharges in 3 min (configurable); not usable in his first 2–3 minutes. Burns his own mouth (heals over 40 s).
- Swims in deep water with plates showing; head periodically surfaces to glare. **In the water the Shinsei Maru boat is his main target.**
- Only naval mines hurt him; at zero health he collapses, lies down `downedSeconds` (15; also **Lies Downed** in the in-game config screen), then regenerates. Exceptions: while his mouth is burnt after a breath, a Shinden crashed into his **face** defeats him outright; and during his 4 s **in-breath** before the beam, a Shinden brings him down in **one hit** (crash, kamikaze near him, or a Torrid burst; `shindenInhaleKnockout`).

## Godzilla Minus Zero (`godzillaminusone:godzilla_minus_zero`)
- Minus One brought down → collapses, lies downed, then exactly one Minus Zero rises in his place (same spot and facing, full health = Minus One's max, targets whoever brought him down). Pending evolution saved in NBT (`Defeat`), so reloads neither lose nor duplicate it; blows while down do nothing; /kill doesn't evolve. Config `evolvesIntoMinusZero` (on).
- Exactly twice Minus One's standing height (45.37 → 90.74 blocks), hitbox 20×80; `bodyScale()` doubles every body-relative point/reach (locators: mouth/beam origin, claws, face, feet, tail sweep, swim depth, bite reach). All numbers, moves, AI, sounds, config identical to Minus One.
- Brought down he collapses and dies for good (no evolution, no regeneration). Own spawn egg (charcoal/grey-green, creative only). His look (tools/make_minus_zero.py, the mod's own design): Minus One's model plus a second staggered row of tilted plates on the lower back and tail (glowing in the charge), armour bands on the arms and layered shoulder caps, spines along the tail's sides, every plate drawn to a sharp point (the tail tip longest), thicker thighs and shins (inflated), darker skin; nothing above Minus One's top, so exactly 2× tall. Own animation file (tools/make_minus_zero_animations.py: Minus One's + `charge_zero`, `tail_strike`).
- Second blast (`surges()`): beam at tick 304, 1st explosion 344; the middle row's glow dims 2 s (to 384) and stays out; the side rows (bones `T12_side`, `T15_side`..`T22_side`, the flanking plates moved off the plate bones: same parent/pivot/rotation) light (renderer `SIDES` pass, `sidePlateTimes`, sharper blue) and punch up one by one from T22 forward, 0.1 s apart, from 384 to `SURGE_TICK` 424; they slam down and the second beam fires (1.35× wider, hits every 2 ticks), 2nd explosion at `SECOND_BLAST_TICK` 464; beam ends `SECOND_BEAM_END_TICK` 508, then the roar (`chargeLength()`). Each explosion `blastScale()` 2: every radius ×2 and the mushroom cloud ×2 (FxPacket b = ±scale). Burns after the beam on mouth, throat and chest (`godzilla_minus_zero_burns*.png`). His own sounds (sound keyframes in `charge_zero`/`tail_strike`, so a played Minus Zero has them too): `godzilla_minus_zero.charged_2nd_time` 0.6 s before the second beam, `.2nd_fire_beam` as it fires (replacing the roar there), `.dorsal_fin_punch_up` as the tail strike starts, `.tail_swing` (synthesized whoosh) at 1.4 s, before the sweep lands.
- Tail strike (`tailStrikes()`): every other spin; T22 punches up and glows (`DATA_TAIL_GLOW`, renderer TAIL_TIP pass: a white-hot core and three ever bigger, bluer additive halos, `TAIL_HALO`) 1 s, then the spin and whip at `TAIL_STRIKE_POWER` 2× damage: living things hurt and flung, armor stands/boats/other entities destroyed (manned Shinden explode, Shinsei Maru wrecked), and every non-liquid breakable block from his feet up in the sweep (`Destruction.tailSweep(..., everything)`, nearest first: 20,000 blocks at once, the rest 8,000 a tick until it's all cleared; breaksBlocks + mobGriefing). Spectators are passed by.

## Rivals: Minus Zero and King Ghidorah (`entity.Rivals`)
- Within `Rivals.RANGE` (160) each targets the other first (Minus Zero `preferRival` every 5 ticks, before boats/planes; King Ghidorah in his tick, not while flying); not with NoAI. Minus Zero's first sight of him (`rivalSeen`): roar (`ROAR_TICKS`), then `breathAfterRoar` starts the charge the tick it ends, ignoring cooldown, first-breath delay, anger delay and minTargetDistance.
- Minus One and King Ghidorah: `Rivals.foes` (any GodzillaEntity vs King Ghidorah) for `blow` shares; either Godzilla brought down by King Ghidorah (`die` with his source): first `Defeat.RECOVER` (lies `GHIDORAH_DOWNED_TICKS` 1200 then EMERGING, whatever evolve/regenerate/Minus Zero's FALL say; `ghidorahDowns` saved), then `Defeat.FALL`. One breath at King Ghidorah per Godzilla (`breathedAtGhidorah`, set when the beam starts at him, saved): after it `holdsBreathAt` him. `ignoreExplosion()` while onlyHurtByNavalMines. Beam bone B shortened by `getBeamReach`: its X/Y/Z offset all scaled with the reach (Y wasn't: a short beam started at his chest).
- King Ghidorah's gravity beams (not the lift): `level.explode` power 4.5 (MOB interaction when breaksBlocks) at each firing head's strike point every 4 ticks.
- Minus Zero never breathes at a Shinden (`holdsBreathAt`: target a ShindenEntity or riding one): no charge starts, one under way is cancelled before the beam; preferRival still comes first.
- King Ghidorah vs an airborne Shinden (`airbornePlane`: piloted, not on the ground, nothing solid 7 below): `HuntGoal` starts `ACTION_PURSUE` (flight TAKEOFF > `CHASE`: towards the plane's position + 6 ticks of its motion, body `CHASE_BELOW` 40 under it, `CHASE_SPEED` 2.2; within `CHASE_REACH` 22 he bites it: `plane.hurt(mobAttack, 100)` > explode; `PURSUE_TICKS` 600, cooldown +100). Every 5 ticks in CHASE, `Rivals.nearest` > target Minus Zero and LAND.
- Minus Zero follows a retreating King Ghidorah: `setTarget` refuses to drop him (or swap him) while `isRetreating()` and within `CHASE_RANGE` (480); `ChaseGoal` walks straight at him. King Ghidorah's `HuntGoal` holds still, facing him, while Minus Zero `isBreathCharging()` (before `BEAM_START_TICK`). Carried (`isPassenger`), Minus Zero's charge is cancelled (`cancelBreath`: also called on King Ghidorah's grab); a charge only starts `onGround()` and not a passenger.
- Damage between them (`Rivals.blow` in both `hurt`s, before the naval-mine-only rule): `maxHealth / 20` an attack, ×4 for heavy ones (Minus Zero `ATOMIC_BREATH`; King Ghidorah `ACTION_BEAM`/`LIFT`/`GRAB`), dealt as damage type `godzillaminusone:kaiju` (bypasses armour). One blow per attack: keyed by attacker + attack id (Minus Zero: `attackSerial` per `performAttack`, or charge start ×2 + which beam; King Ghidorah: action start << 4 | action). Radiation heat and footstep crush aren't attacks. `Blasts.nuclearHit` passes Minus Zero's beam/blast to King Ghidorah's `hurt`.
- Minus Zero's dorsal plates: points `TIP_LENGTH` 0.35 of the plate (was 0.5), `TIP_DEPTH` 0.65; T22's 0.75 + 0.4 (was 1.0 + 0.6). Then the owner's Blockbench edits (`tools/owner_edits.py`, data in `tools/owner_edits/`: `<model>.geo.edits.json` = whole bones as the owner left them, laid over the generated ones; `<texture>.paint.json` = repainted texels): Minus Zero 31 bones (plates taller, tips and tail/arm spines removed, T22 moved (0, 2, 3) and its side plates `T22_side` with it, jaw `Jew` 5°, head eye cubes 0.4 in) + 120 texels (eyes yellow/black); Minus One's Head (eye cubes 0.3 in) + 8 texels; applied by `make_minus_zero.py` (`owners()`, after its height check) and `convert_addon.py`. King Ghidorah's (knee spurs off, eyes: black slit pupil, white glint) are in `make_ghidorah.py` itself.

## King Ghidorah (`godzillaminusone:king_ghidorah`)
- The mod's own three-headed golden dragon (`tools/make_ghidorah.py`: geo, 512 px texture at 2 texels/unit like Godzilla's, 14 animations). 100 bones; each wing membrane on two bones (`wing_webN` turned+scaled > `wing_webN_skin` turned) solved per frame (SVD of the map pinning its pivot and two anchors) so membranes follow any wing pose. Stance (image 1): crouched, leaning on his wings like front legs, wrist claws planted on the ground (0.3-0.5 units up), wings half spread, never folded (tucked only in the entrance); walk on all fours, each wing stepping with the opposite hind leg (`arm_step`: a linear map of back/lift to shoulder/upper/fore angles). Clips: idle, walk, roar, bite_c/_r/_l, stomp, tail_whip, gravity_beam, gravity_lift, fly, fly_carry, death, entrance (14.6 s, body raised from below the surface by the root bone's position).
- As tall as Minus Zero to his heads (`RENDER_SCALE` = 90.74 × 16 / 105.5 units), Minus Zero's hitbox 20×80, maxHealth/attackDamage from the Godzilla config, only naval mines hurt him (`hitByNavalMine`, mineDamagePercent), nuclear blasts don't (`Blasts`), calm regen, Godzilla's roar interval and targeting (+ Godzilla). Walk-through destruction from walkBreaksFromY.
- Attacks (`HuntGoal`): bite (nearest head, tick 12), stomp (tick 17, footprint), tail whip (tick 15, `Destruction.tailSweep` 8-66), gravity beams 40-160 blocks (heads fire ticks 24/27/30 until 136, 5.6 s to match the beam sound; zap every 8 ticks; not stopped by blocks; `Vaporize` removes every breakable non-liquid block within 12 of each strike plus a radius-2.5 bore along the last 40 blocks of path, 2500 a tick; entities on the beam or within 12 of the strike take attackDamage), lift (beams hold the target, 0.95 up a tick to 34 blocks, then release), grab (flight: takeoff, approach, `startRiding` in his talons (re-gripped if dismounted), ascend to 48 + its height, dive, slam: attackDamage + explosion without block damage, land). Retreat (config `retreats`, `retreatHealthPercent` 20, in-game toggle): flies 220 blocks away and lands, target dropped, 2 min cooldown. Flight: noGravity + noPhysics, synced `DATA_FLYING`/`DATA_CARRYING`. Landing: `Destruction.digDitch` (queued `Ditch`, columns nearest first, 20000 block looks a tick; `ditch` digs at once for the tests) (floor radius 15, radius 26, depth 5; ragged ±10% edge, smoothstep sides, rubble floor, rim heap 3 out, clears 24 up, fluid columns skipped, not limited by walkBreaksFromY), then he drops into it.
- Walk: `plant_walk` in make_ghidorah.py solves thigh/shin per frame (Newton on the ankle's y/z) so each hind foot is planted flat 60% of the stride (`WALK_STANCE`), sliding back at 0.35 blocks/tick, lifted 5 units in the swing; toes 0.3-0.6 units up when down. Server walk clock (`walkClockTick` on both sides) presses footfalls at `WALK_TOUCHDOWNS` 3/27: shake, STOMP step damage (stepDamage/stepRadius), `Destruction.footprint` at `walkFoot` (±11.4, 0, 7.5 units; half 5.2 × 8.1 units). `setDigs(false)` for the game tests.
- Entrance: once per spawn (finalizeSpawn: egg, /summon without NBT; NBT `EntranceDone`, a reload mid-entrance resumes it), on solid ground, invulnerable, fixed in place, AI off. Server sounds by tick (rumble, cracks, footsteps at the lurches, wing spread, entrance roar). Client effects (`client.GhidorahEffects` via `EffectHooks.ghidorah`) by the server's clock: radial cracks with ground-matched block particles, bursts, fountains where heads emerge, dust clouds, wing dust rush, falling earth, camera shake scaled by client `ghidorahEntranceShake` (0-100). At tick 160 (`ENTRANCE_HOLE_AT`) `Destruction.digDitch` digs his hole (floor radius 24, radius 50, depth 13) and he sinks into it (`sinkTick`: setPos down as the ground under him goes, AI off too). Weather: `entranceStormDelayTicks` (60) after the entrance ends, `GhidorahWeather.start` (SavedData `Until`; game weather kept clear; lightning at `STRIKE_ONE_IN` 50000 per ticking chunk per tick = 2x vanilla's 100000, around players within simulation distance, at living things under the sky as vanilla; `GhidorahWeatherPacket` on start/end/login/dimension change; client `GhidorahSky` fades 100 ticks; `mixin.ClientLevelMixin` greys `getSkyColor`/`getCloudColor`, dims `getSkyDarken`; fog greyed), skylight + no ceiling only.
- Sync: `DATA_ACTION_START` (game time) drives `getActionTicks()` on both sides; `ServerClockController` (GeckoLib controller subclass overriding `adjustTick`) plays action clips at the server's time, so late watchers see the right moment.
- Beams: `KingGhidorahRenderer` draws each as a jagged bolt (kinks every ~5 blocks, re-struck every 2 ticks) in `RenderType.eyes(king_ghidorah_beam.png)` glow layers + an alpha-blended gold body, plus 3 forking bolts; server `lightBeams` keeps `Blocks.LIGHT` (15) in air every 7 blocks along firing beams and by the strike (moved every 2 ticks, removed on stop/remove, saved as `BeamLights` and cleared on load).
- Model detail (make_ghidorah.py): membranes web1-5 (web5: elbow, finger3 tip, flank; web3 now W-T3-E, web4 S-E-flank: shared edges all between pins), neck side scale rods + second spike row, more crown spikes and snout knobs, belly/breast scutes, flank ridges, tail side spikes on odd segments, wing arm spurs/forearm plates/knuckle spikes, thigh scales (no knee spurs: the owner took them off). 104 bones, 993 cubes.
- Client: `KingGhidorahModel` per-head wandering gaze (off in the entrance, damped while busy); `KingGhidorahRenderer` draws concentrated golden beams (layered: additive glow, alpha-blended gold body, additive white core; crackling bolts round them; a flare at the strike) from the real `mouth_*` bone positions.
- Sounds from the owner-supplied King Ghidorah sound pack (mono, source/licence not given): `king_ghidorah.entrance_roar` (entrance tick 250), `.wing_span_roar` (roar), `.idle` (ambient, not in the entrance), `.hurt`, `.death`, `.bite` (bite tick 7, grab seize), `.gravity_beam` (once at the middle head's fire tick, beam and lift), `.flying` (every 164 action ticks in flight), `godzilla.stomp` for his stomp (as Godzilla's: volume 12 × 2, pitch 0.95-1.05), `.footstep_1/_2/_3` (in turn: walk via a client walk clock (`walkClockTick`, advancing at strideSpeed) that also plays the walk clip (`WalkClockController.adjustTick`), footsteps at `WALK_FOOTFALLS` 1/25 of 48 ticks (feet touch at 3/27), pace = client ground speed / 0.35, heard even when he isn't rendered (GeckoLib keyframes only fire when drawn); landing/entrance lurches via a server counter; vanilla step sound off). Synthesized (`make_sounds.py`): `.rumble`, `.earth_crack`, `.wing_spread`. Spawn egg (gold/burgundy). Model proportions studied from "King Ghidorah" by I HATE YOU WINDOWS XP (Sketchfab, CC-BY 4.0); nothing copied.

## The atomic blast (visuals)
Film-accurate "Ginza blast" sequence: flash, fireball, dust ring/shockwave, rising column, mushroom cap (built from Alex's Caves cloud textures), cooling, mature cloud, slow break-up (~3 min). Fully configurable timings/size.

## Odo Island Monster (`godzillaminusone:odo_island_monster`)
- Godzilla's earlier dinosaur form (Monster122's Godzillasaurus model), 14 blocks tall, 600 HP.
- Charges players within ~16 blocks; frenzies when seen/hurt (faster, harder bites/stomps).
- **Goes through buildings**: anything built (short of obsidian) gives as he walks into it; blocked on the way to someone he tears down toward them, and against a wall he bites through it. Polished/cut stone and bricks count as built, not rock.
- **Spotlight reaction:** caught by a manned searchlight's beam he stares into it, roars, charges and smashes it, then hunts its operator. If the light is on/in a building he **tears the building down** to get it. A player coming within 12 blocks distracts him onto them.
- Hit by a nuclear bomb he's burnt, lies dormant 3 minutes, then **rises as Godzilla**.
- Sounds: roar, searchlight-spotted shriek, spawn cry (alone: no vanilla ambient growl or roar over it), bite, nuked cry, distant roar (100+ blocks). Vanilla-style spawn egg. Never gets stuck going for a light (tears through obstacles; gives up after 30 s and hunts the operator).

## Hazmat suit
Hood, suit, trousers, boots (GeckoLib armour, detailed: blue suit, black full-face mask and big visor, harness with yellow detector and gauge, two white cylinders with red diamond labels, gloves, yellow-green soled boots). All four worn: no Radiation, existing Radiation removed, radiation damage cancelled.

## Searchlight
WWII searchlight, mountable. Rider aims it by looking; **R** toggles the lamp. Unmanned it **sweeps automatically like a clock** (doesn't anger Odo). Beam is a soft glow round a core, each stretch facing the camera and fading when seen end-on (no glare in the middle of the operator's view), with a block-by-block lit pool (on the surface over water); fades narrow the strips to nothing (not just alpha) and beam/pool fade into the fog, so shader packs (Iris/Oculus) show no line in the middle of the view or pools hanging in the sky; drawn after water/ice so it doesn't x-ray through them. Mobs anywhere in a lit beam (inside the cone, short of where it lands, in sight of the lens) are drawn lit, client side, via a mixin on EntityRenderDispatcher.getPackedLightCoords.

## Shinsei Maru (boat)
- The film's run-down minesweeper, **16.3 × 5.6 blocks**, heavily weathered (rust streaks, flaking paint, tyre fenders, nets, rigging).
- **Walkable solid decks** (overlapping small tiles: support at any heading, incl. the wheelhouse roof), you're carried as she moves; **ladders** on both sides and up the wheelhouse to the roof helm.
- **Two seats:** driver (wheel on wheelhouse roof; W/S/A/D; **R** drops naval mines from her 9-slot hold) and gunner (Type 93 machine gun at the stern, **automatic while holding right-click**). Both crew stand at their stations.
- Sits steady (no bobbing of her own). Physics Mod 3.0.20 moves every entity it draws on its own waves (EntityOcean, mixed into all entities, applied in EntityRenderDispatcher), so she should ride them where its ocean runs; not seen working (its ocean never came up in CI's software-rendered game). Quiet synthesized diesel motor loop while under way. Godzilla prioritises her in water.

## Other items/entities
- **Kyushu J7W1 Shinden** fighter plane: flyable, hold right-click for cannons, explodes near Godzilla (film ending) or on crash. Automatic third person framing the plane while piloting; engine silent while parked on the ground; hold Left Ctrl to free-look without steering.
- **Naval Mine** (craftable; also in end city 25%, nether fortress 15%, ancient city 20% chests, 1–3 per chest weighted 6:3:1, 3 rarest) + **Remote Detonator**. Only thing that damages Godzilla.
- **Nuclear Bomb** item: called down at a target, 20 s air-raid siren, same blast as the atomic breath.
- **Godzilla statues** (summon only, for thumbnails): `godzilla_pose_charging_roar`, `_tail_whip`, `_sky_roar`, `_poster_snarl` (70th anniversary poster, glowing mouth and plates), `_tail_hook`, `_hunched_snarl` (square-on, hunched, head low between the shoulders).

## Playing as Godzilla (optional Walkers / ReMorphed)
- Unlock via Walkers (unlock key / `/walkers change2ndShape`) or ReMorphed (defeat him or `/remorphed addShape`); Godzilla Minus Zero too (`godzillaminusone:godzilla_minus_zero`; bringing him down credits ReMorphed). As Minus Zero every reach is ×`bodyScale()` (2), his breath is his (`chargeLength()`, second beam and blast, `blastScale()` 2) and he has **X** Tail Strike (`Move.TAIL_STRIKE`: T22 glow on the shape, then `GodzillaEntity.strikeAround` + `Destruction.tailSweep(..., everything)` at 2× damage).
- Forced third-person camera pulled back to frame his whole body.
- Moves with a small top-left HUD: **Z** Tail Whip, **H** Scratch, **J** Bite, **K** Roar (weakness/slowness in 48 blocks), **B** Atomic Breath (full charge + nuclear blast, 3-min recharge; head follows your look).
- Every move is also a command for the player playing as him (`GodzillaCommands`): `/godzilla tailwhip|scratch|bite|roar|breath|tailstrike` (tailstrike only as Minus Zero; same waits and recharge as the keys).
- The six keys are ordinary key bindings (Options > Controls > Key Binds > Godzilla Minus One; saved in options.txt; the HUD shows the bound keys), active only while you're Godzilla (`ModKeyContexts.GODZILLA_SHAPE`), whether Walkers or ReMorphed made you him.
- Walking destroys what's in his way at Y ≥ `walkBreaksFromY` (60), nothing below, and leaves footprints (`playable` config section); tail whip and scratch tear down buildings. J is a real bite. Rooted from breath charge to the end of the post-beam roar; any interruption frees you.
- Never stuck: **M** (or the config screen) switches **Auto-Climb** (steps up to `playerGodzillaStepHeight`, 20 blocks) and **Walk Through** (passes through any block; mixins on Entity.collide / LocalPlayer.moveTowardsClosestSpace, server noPhysics between ticks, no suffocation).

## Playing as the Odo Island Monster (optional)
- Moves at 12 blocks/s with no key (`playerOdoBlocksPerSecond`; sprint adds nothing); forced third person framing him; left click bites.
- Note: Walkers' ability key is also R (conflicts with the mod's "Vehicle Action"). Shinden: hold Left Ctrl for free look.
- Checked in the released game with Walkers 5.8.13, ReMorphed 4.2, CraftedCore 5.8.2 (mod ids walkers, remorphed, craftedcore): all eight mod keys in Controls once; Godzilla's Z/H/J/K/B each making its move, Roar rebound to N working and saved in options.txt, rooted through B's charge and roar then free, breaking through a building, changing back; the Odo (taken through ReMorphed's morph request) running 12.00 blocks/s in three 20-tick stretches with one speed modifier, removed on letting go, speed back to 0.1 (4.32 blocks/s walking) after changing back; left-click bite.
- Note: before this version the keys were registered by four same-named `Keys.register` handlers that Forge's event bus merged into one, so only Vehicle Action was in Controls; `client.ModKeyMappings` now registers all of them.

## Config
In-game config screen (health bar, breath/roar intervals, Godzilla's downed time, played Godzilla's Auto-Climb/Walk Through, blast visuals, King Ghidorah retreats, his entrance's camera shake) plus `godzillaminusone-common.toml` (kill/radiation radius, `craterRadius` 48, `scorchRadius` 60, `ruinRadius` 100, `flattenRadius` 60, `flattenDownToY` 65, building demolition (`demolishFromHeight`/`demolishDownToHeight` -1 = auto, `demolitionIntervalSeconds`), `downedSeconds`, `minTargetDistance`, playable-shape options, block breaking, mortality, etc.).

## Physics Mod
No Physics Mod API is called (only its presence is detected). Building toppling is the mod's own falling-block simulation; other breaking uses vanilla block breaking, which Physics Mod shatters by itself (with it installed, all demolition breaks go that way and the mod adds no extra particles). Its waves are its own client-side business for every entity (see the boat above). All in-game checks pass in the released game with Physics Mod 3.0.20 installed.
